Mexican Orange Blossom

Nature:Popular evergreen shrub known for its aromatic, glossy green leaves and clusters of fragrant white flowers. These bloom in late spring.

Size:Plants reach a height of 6 to 8 feet (1.8 to 2.4 m), with aa spread of about 5 to 6 feet (1.5 to 1.8 m)..

Habitat:Partially shaded location, that is protected from strong winds..

Prune:Trim only if needed to restrict or re-shape growth, or to shorten leggy stems. Prune in early to mid summer, immediately after flowering.

Box Hedge

Nature:Popular evergreen hedge, bright green leaves, versatile

Size:As a guide, Buxus sempervirens (Common Box) hedging can actually grow to 8m or even higher but this is still the choice for small hedges as it can be pruned easily to any shape and size.

Habitat: They are tolerant to most soil types and do well in full sun, partial sun and partial shade,.

Prune:Prune box hedge twice a year during the growing season from April to September.

Wisteria

Nature:Wisteria is a genus of flowering plants in the legume family, Fabaceae.

Size: Seed dispersal.

Habitat:Wisteria plants prefer a sunny or semi-shaded site in moist, well drained soil. Wisteria flower buds can be damaged by frost, so choose a sheltered spot if possible..

Prune:Prune twice a year, first in summer after flowering and second in late winter when dormant.

Bay Tree

Nature:The bay tree is a popular evergreen shrub suitable for containers or growing in the ground.

Size:Up to 7.5m (23ft) unless clipped.

Habitat:Full sun or partial shade.

Prune:Trimming your topiary bay in the summer not only maintains its attractive shape, but also encourages denser growth. .
