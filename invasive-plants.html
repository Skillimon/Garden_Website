Bamboo

Nature: Green leaves shaped like a lance or spear that taper to a point. Stem/culm rounded and thick. Tallest bamboo can grow up to 40 metres.

How it spreads: Via shallow rhizomes (runners).

Treatment: To get rid of bamboo, the most effective method is to dig up the entire root system (rhizomes) and canes, making sure to remove all fragments to prevent regrowth.

Montbretia (Crocosmia x Crocosmiiflora)

Nature: Grass-like leaves, bright orange flowers.

How it spreads: Although it produces seeds, most reproduction is vegetative.

Treatment: Persistent digging or systemic weed killer.

Cotoneaster

Nature: Woody, shrub-like stems, short thick leaves, and small toxic berries & flowers. Many varieties exist, but only 5 are considered invasive in the UK.

How it spreads: Seed dispersal.

Treatment: Persistent digging or systemic weed killer.
